# Research Routing

Research when an external fact could materially change a requirement, constraint, recommendation, or decision.

Pattern:
1. state the Research Question,
2. identify authoritative/relevant sources,
3. capture Findings with provenance and limitations,
4. seek meaningful counter-evidence,
5. stop when additional evidence is unlikely to change the decision.

Research is evidence, not project truth. Translate findings into options or implications; do not auto-promote them to requirements.
