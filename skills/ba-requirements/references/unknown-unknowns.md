# Unknown-Unknown Coverage

Before the phase gate, scan for plausible decision-sensitive blind spots using the project's domain and artifacts, not a generic giant questionnaire.

Check only relevant categories such as:
- exception/error flows,
- authority/approval and overrides,
- volume/latency or timing boundaries,
- data ownership and source of truth,
- integration/dependency assumptions,
- security/privacy/regulatory constraints,
- migration/compatibility,
- operational recovery/auditability.

A blind spot becomes a requirement only after evidence and stakeholder validation justify it.
