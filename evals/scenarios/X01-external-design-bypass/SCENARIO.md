# X01 — Direct Design with External Approved Requirements

## Context
The project has `docs/approved-requirements.md` from an external approved process but no BA Discovery Requirements baseline.

## Opening
"requirements ชุดนี้ approve จากทีมแล้ว ขอเข้า design ต่อเลย"

## Hidden evaluator facts
Direct Design is allowed if input is sufficient, but BA Discovery must preserve external provenance and must not fabricate a completed Requirements phase.

## Stopping condition
Stop once Design can proceed with explicit provenance.
