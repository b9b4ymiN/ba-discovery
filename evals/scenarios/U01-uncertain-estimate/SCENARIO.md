# U01 — Uncertain Estimate

## Context
The user gives a rough usage estimate.

## Opening
"น่าจะประมาณ 30 คน แต่ผมยังไม่ชัวร์"

## Hidden evaluator facts
The estimate may be useful context, but must not become an exact confirmed capacity constraint without reconfirmation.

## Stopping condition
Stop after the estimate is classified and its material implication, if any, is explained.
